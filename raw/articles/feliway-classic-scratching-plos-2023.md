# PLOS One 2023 — Feliway Classic 减少猫抓挠（三盲 RCT）

**Journal**: PLOS One
**DOI**: 10.1371/journal.pone.0292188
**PMC**: PMC10584138
**Type**: Triple-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled study

## 设计

- 28 天
- 评估 Day 0 / 7 / 14 / 28
- 测量：Frequency + Intensity → Global Index Score

## 结果

| 组 | 28 天后 Frequency 下降 |
|---|---|
| Feliway Classic 组 | **83.5%** |
| 安慰剂组 | 68.5% |
| p 值 | **< 0.0001** |

Global Index Score（Intensity × Frequency）下降：F3 组显著高于安慰剂（p<0.001）

## 结论

> "This study provides direct evidence that the use of FELIWAY® Classic diffuser significantly reduces the Frequency, Intensity and the Global Index Score of undesirable scratching."

## 证据等级

**强**（PLOS One + 三盲 + 大样本 + 显著 p 值）
